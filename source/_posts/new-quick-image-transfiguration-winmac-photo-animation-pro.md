### 7\. [Gimp](https://www.gimp.org/)

### 8\. [Sketch Me!](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/p/sketch-me/9nblgggzshg4?activetab=pivot:overviewtab)

### 10\. [Pixelmator Pro](https://www.pixelmator.com/pro/)
